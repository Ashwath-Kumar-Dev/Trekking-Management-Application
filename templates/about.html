🥾 Trekking Management System

Explore. Book. Trek. Enjoy.

About Our Application

This application helps customers discover and book trekking packages. Staff members manage assigned trekking activities, while trekkers can view available treks and track their bookings.

System Roles

👨‍💼 Admin

Manages treks, users, bookings and staff members.

🥾 Trek Staff

Manages assigned treks, participants and trekking activities.

🧑‍🎓 Trekker

Browses available treks, makes bookings and tracks booking history.
